Your Day,
Simplified

Mavee brings your calendar, mail, contacts, tasks, files and health together and shows what needs you today — two meetings that collide, a bill due, a flight with no calendar entry, the document to re-read before a meeting. Then your day, laid out with the mail and files behind each meeting. Then search that answers.

Three Screens, Three Questions

Each one answers something you would otherwise piece together from four apps.

Today

What needs me?

  • Scheduling conflicts across every calendar, with the colliding calendars named
  • Bills and invoices due, travel emails with no calendar entry, a document you edited before a related meeting, birthdays this week, a short night before a heavy day
  • Each with one tap: Remind Me, Later, or Dismiss — and dismissed stays dismissed
  • What's next, last night's sleep and today's steps, open to-dos pulled from your mail, today's weather
My Day

What does my day look like?

  • All your calendars in one hour-by-hour grid, overlapping events side by side
  • Open time with a suggestion: a walk, focus work, lunch, prep, a travel buffer
  • Meeting prep: the recent emails and documents that relate to each meeting, gathered under it
  • Swipe between days; conflicts ringed on the grid
Search

Where is that?

  • Instant results across mail, calendar, files, contacts, tasks and health as you type
  • Ask a question — "what's my next interview?" — and get an answer built only from your own data, with the items it used
  • Pin a search as a Topic and see what's new since you last looked
  • Works from Spotlight and Siri too

Your Accounts, Your Device

Mavee reads from the accounts you already have. Data is fetched straight to your device and stays there.

AppleCalendar (every calendar, shared ones included), Reminders, Contacts, Health — steps, sleep, workouts.
Microsoft 365Outlook Mail, Calendar, Contacts, OneDrive, To Do. More than one account if you need it.
GoogleGmail, Calendar, Contacts, Drive — including the text inside your documents.
SpotifyRecently played, so your listening shows up in search.

Get Started in Minutes

Four onboarding steps. No account to create.

1

Allow Apple Services

Calendar, Reminders, Contacts and Health — each one asks only when you switch it on.

2

Connect Accounts

Sign in to Microsoft 365, Google or Spotify on one page, or skip and try a demo week first.

3

See Your Day

Mavee updates in the background and whenever you open it. Today shows what needs you, My Day shows your schedule, Search finds the rest.

Nothing Leaves Your Device

Mavee has no server and no account of its own. What it finds, it finds on your phone.

✅ On-Device Processing ✅ No Cloud Sync ✅ No Analytics ✅ No Tracking ✅ No Advertising

Conflicts, signals and meeting prep are plain logic on the device. Apple's on-device model is used in two places only: to answer a question in Search and to pull to-dos out of your mail — and Settings keeps a log of every time it ran. Erase everything from Settings at any time; events older than a year are pruned on their own.

What you need: iOS 26 or macOS 26. Answers and to-do extraction need a device with Apple Intelligence; everything else works without it.
