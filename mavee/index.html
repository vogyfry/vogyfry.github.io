Your Day, Simplified · iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad & Mac · On Device

Your life is in six apps.
What needs you is in one.

Mavee brings your calendar, mail, contacts, tasks, files and health together on your device and reads them for the things worth your attention: two meetings that collide, a bill due, a flight with no calendar entry, the mail and files behind your next meeting. It taps you on the shoulder fifteen minutes before that meeting, lays out your day, and answers anything you ask.

Everything Happens on Your Device

Mavee has no server and no account of its own. What it reads, it reads on your phone; what it finds, it finds there too.

Ready for iPhone Duo

Built on the iOS 27.2 SDK, so it fills the Duo's displays edge to edge. Mavee lays itself out by how much room it has, not by which way the phone is turned, and opening the phone is just a resize.

Closed

A Phone, One Column

On the outer display Today is the phone layout, with the Today, My Day and Search tabs and the Settings button in a strip along the edge, where iOS puts them.

Open

Two Columns, Nothing Stretched

Open it and Today splits: what needs you and today's context on one side, what's next, your health strip and your to-dos on the other. My Day's grid gets the width; Search keeps its field in view.

Fold

Open, Close, Keep Reading

The layout follows the fold in place. Verified in Apple's iPhone Duo simulator, closed and open, with the same tests that run on iPhone and iPad.

What You Need

Mavee is in TestFlight for iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad, with the same screens on Mac.

System

iOS 26 or macOS 26

Built for iOS 26's Liquid Glass and Apple's Foundation Models framework. Ready for iPhone Duo, closed or open. iPad shows the same app; Mac runs it in a sidebar window with Settings in the usual place.

For answers

Apple Intelligence

The answer card, the question chips, the Your Day summary and to-do extraction use the on-device model, so they need a device with Apple Intelligence turned on. Without it, every card, the heads-up and My Day work, and the chips become plain searches.

Accounts

Whatever You Already Use

Nothing is required. Apple's own apps are enough to start; Microsoft 365, Google and Spotify are optional and can be added any time in Settings.
