Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Waypoint is built on a simple principle: where you're going is your business. Your location, your saved trips and what you type all stay on your iPhone, and reliability is scored on the device. There is no Waypoint account, no Waypoint server, no analytics and no advertising.

1. What We Don't Collect

Waypoint does not collect, store, or transmit to us:

  • Your location or the trips you take
  • Your destinations, or the requests you type (such as "ferry building by 8:45")
  • Your saved trips and rider preferences
  • Analytics or usage telemetry
  • Crash reports sent to any server of ours
  • Device identifiers, fingerprints, or advertising identifiers

2. Location

Waypoint asks for your location while you're using the app, to time the walk to your first stop and to tell you the moment to leave. Location is processed on the device and is never sent to Prava Labs or to any transit agency. You can use the app without granting location; you then choose where you're starting from yourself.

3. On-Device Processing

Trip planning and reliability scoring run on your iPhone, against arrival history that is summarised by us and bundled into the app. When you type a request in plain language, Waypoint first reads it with built-in rules. If those can't settle where you're going, it may ask Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence, where your device supports it). That model runs on your iPhone, and what you typed is not sent to Prava Labs.

4. Connections Waypoint Makes

Waypoint makes network connections only to:

  • 511 SF Bay — the regional transit data service run by the Metropolitan Transportation Commission, for live vehicle positions and predictions. Requests are made by transit operator and carry nothing about you: not your location, your destination or your trip. As with any web request, 511's servers see your device's IP address.
  • Apple — when you search for a destination by name, the search goes to Apple Maps. Apple's handling of those searches is described in Apple's privacy policy, not ours. Apple also delivers the app and its updates.

Waypoint never routes your data through Prava Labs servers.

5. Your iPhone, Apple Watch, Widgets and Live Activities

Your saved trips and preferences are stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone, in a container shared only with Waypoint's widgets and Live Activities on the same device. The trip you're on is passed from your iPhone to Waypoint on your paired Apple Watch directly, using Apple's device-to-device connection. None of this passes through Prava Labs, and deleting the app deletes it.

6. Notifications

Waypoint asks permission to send notifications so it can tell you when to leave, or when a connection is at risk. Notifications are generated on the device; no push service of ours is involved.

7. Third-Party Services

Waypoint does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services. There are no SDKs, no beacons, no pixel tracking.

8. TestFlight Beta

If you test Waypoint through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share with us the information TestFlight collects: the email address or name you were invited with, crash reports, device model and OS version, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. We use this only to fix and improve Waypoint, and do not share it with anyone else. Apple's collection is governed by Apple's TestFlight terms and privacy policy.

9. Purchase and Payment

App Store purchases, if any, are processed entirely by Apple; we do not receive or store any payment information.

10. Support Communications

If you contact us at support@pravalabs.app, we receive only the information you choose to include in your email. We use it solely to respond and do not share it with any third party.

11. Children's Privacy

Waypoint is a transit planning tool not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at privacy@pravalabs.app.
