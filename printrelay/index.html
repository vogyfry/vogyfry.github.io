Prava PrintRelay

Print from your iPhone and iPad to any printer your Mac can use.

Prava PrintRelay is a menu bar app for macOS 26 and later that shares the printers set up on your Mac as AirPrint printers. USB printers, older network printers and printers that need a vendor driver all show up on your iPhone, iPad, Android phone, Chromebook or another Mac, with nothing to install on them.

  • Flip a switch per printer. Paper sizes, duplex and colour come from the real printer.
  • No terminal, no Printer Sharing toggle, no admin password.
  • Sandboxed Mac App Store app. Print jobs never leave your network.
  • One purchase, no subscription, with a free trial built in.
