Most requested

The Things People Ask For, Built In

These are the features people ask for most from a Mac printer-sharing app. PrintRelay has every one.

📄

Save to Your Mac as a PDF

The feature people ask for first: print from the iPhone, get a PDF in a folder on your Mac. No AirDrop, no email to yourself.

⚡

Run a Shortcut on Every PDF

OCR it, rename it from its contents, file it away or send it on. One setting turns a PDF printer into a paperless workflow.

🗂️

More Than One PDF Printer

Up to eight, each with its own folder and its own app to open in: Receipts to one folder, Work to another, To Notes straight into Notes.

🍎

Works on the Latest macOS

Built for macOS 26 and sandboxed for the Mac App Store. No kernel extensions, no helper to install, no admin password, nothing that breaks with the next update.

🌙

Still There After Sleep

Each printer keeps the same address and identity across sleep, restarts and updates, so your iPhone finds the same printer every time instead of a new duplicate.

🤖

Android, Chromebook and Windows Too

Shared printers also speak IPP Everywhere, so Android phones, Chromebooks, Windows PCs and other Macs print to them without a driver.

🔐

A Password for Each Printer

Keep guests and neighbours off your printer. A protected printer is shared only over an encrypted connection, so the password and the document never cross the Wi-Fi in the clear. Your iPhone asks once and remembers it.

⚠️

No Duplicate Printers

When macOS Printer Sharing also advertises a printer you share, iPhones list it twice. PrintRelay spots it, warns you in the menu, and opens Sharing settings to fix it.

Your Printer Works on the Mac. Now It Works Everywhere.

A printer that needs a driver, or hangs off a USB cable, never shows up on an iPhone. PrintRelay puts every printer your Mac can use on your network as a standard AirPrint printer.

🔌

Every Printer, One Switch

USB, network and driver-based printers are listed in the menu. Flip a switch and the printer is on your Wi-Fi within seconds. If it prints from the Mac, it prints from the phone.

📄

The Real Printer's Options

Paper sizes, two-sided printing and colour come from the printer's own driver, so the iPhone's Print sheet offers what the printer can actually do.

📱

Nothing to Install on the Phone

No companion app, no profile, no account. Print from any app's Share sheet on iPhone, iPhone Duo or iPad. Your Mac's name is shown under the printer, so you always know which Mac is sharing it.

🔔

Quiet Until It Matters

Lives in the menu bar, launches at login, and notifies you when a job prints, saves or fails. A short activity list in the menu shows what happened.

PDF Printers: Paperless, With a Plan

Add up to eight virtual printers, such as "Save as PDF", "Receipts" or "Work". Each appears on your iPhone by name and saves what you print as a PDF in its own folder on your Mac. Then it does what you chose.

📁

Its Own Folder

Each PDF printer saves into the folder you pick, as "job name, date and time.pdf". Receipts go to Receipts, work goes to Work, without sorting anything later.

👀

Open in Any App

After saving, open the file in Preview, or in any app that opens PDF: Notes, Mail, DEVONthink or your own choice. Or do nothing and let the folder fill up.

⚡

Run a Shortcut

Hand each new PDF to a Shortcut you pick: extract the text, rename it from its contents, file it away, add it to Notes or send it by mail. It runs in the background, and the menu tells you it ran.

Example: a Receipts printer. Add a PDF printer named Receipts and point it at a Receipts folder in Documents. Set After saving to a Shortcut that reads the date and the shop from the PDF's text and renames the file, so it lands as "2026-11-09 Corner Market.pdf". When an order confirmation or e-receipt arrives on your iPhone, tap Share › Print › Receipts. By the time you sit down at the Mac, it's filed and named.

Everything in the Menu Bar

Switch printers on in the menu. Set up PDF printers, passwords and launch at login in Settings.

Set Up in a Minute

No terminal, no Printer Sharing toggle, no admin password.

1

Set Up the Printer on the Mac

If it already prints from your Mac, you're done with this step.

2

Switch It On

Click the printer icon in the menu bar and switch on the printers you want to share. Allow Local Network access when macOS asks.

3

Print From Your iPhone

In any app, tap Share, then Print, and pick the printer. Your Mac's name is shown under it.

4

Add PDF Printers

In Settings › General, click + under Save as PDF, give it a name and choose its folder. Then pick what happens after saving.
