Frequently Asked Questions

What does Prava PrintRelay do?

It makes the printers your Mac can print to show up as AirPrint printers on your iPhone, iPad and other devices on the same Wi-Fi. That includes USB printers, older network printers and printers that need a vendor driver. Nothing is installed on the phone; you print from the normal Share sheet.

How do I set it up?

  1. Make sure the printer is set up on the Mac and prints from the Mac.
  2. Open Prava PrintRelay. It lives in the menu bar; click the printer icon.
  3. Switch on the printer you want to share. Allow local network access when macOS asks.
  4. On the iPhone, open any app, tap Share, then Print, and pick the printer. Your Mac's name is shown under it.

My iPhone cannot see the printer.

  • The Mac must be awake and logged in, and the app must be running. Turn on "Launch at login" in Settings so it is always there.
  • Check System Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network and make sure Prava PrintRelay is allowed. The app shows a warning in its menu when access is off.
  • The iPhone and the Mac must be on the same network. Guest Wi-Fi networks and some mesh systems block the discovery traffic AirPrint uses.
  • If the printer is already an AirPrint printer, the app says "Already supports AirPrint" and there is no need to share it.

I see the printer twice.

Either macOS Printer Sharing is on (System Settings > General > Sharing), or another AirPrint tool is still running on the Mac. Turn the other one off; Prava PrintRelay's entry is the one with your Mac's name under it.

Can other devices print too?

Yes. Shared printers also speak IPP Everywhere, so Android phones, Chromebooks, Windows and other Macs can print to them.

Does it print while I am logged out?

No. The app runs in your user session, so the Mac must be awake with you logged in. This is a limit of Mac App Store apps.

How does the trial and purchase work?

Every printer can be shared during the free trial. After it ends, sharing pauses until you buy the full version in Settings > License, a one-time purchase through the App Store. "Restore Purchases" brings it back on another Mac with the same Apple Account.

The app is misbehaving. What should I do?

  • Make sure you are running the latest version from the App Store.
  • Switch the printer off and on again in the menu, or click Rescan Printers.
  • Quit and relaunch Prava PrintRelay from the menu bar.
  • If issues persist, email support@pravalabs.app with your macOS version, the printer model and how it is connected, and what the menu's activity list shows.
