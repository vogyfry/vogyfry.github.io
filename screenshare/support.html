Frequently Asked Questions

What does ScreenShare do?

ScreenShare shows your Mac's screen on your iPad and lets you control it with a keyboard, trackpad, mouse or touch. It uses the Screen Sharing service built into macOS, so there is nothing to install on the Mac. You can have several Macs open at once, each in its own window.

How do I set it up?

  1. On the Mac, open System Settings > General > Sharing and turn on Screen Sharing.
  2. On the iPad, open ScreenShare and tap Network. Tap the Mac when it appears. Or tap New Connection and enter the Mac's name or IP address.
  3. Sign in with the user name and password you use on that Mac. Turn on Remember Password if you want ScreenShare to keep it in the Keychain.

Which user name and password do I use?

The ones you use to log in to that Mac. If the Mac has "VNC viewers may control screen with password" turned on in its Screen Sharing options, ScreenShare can also sign in with that password alone. A user name and password can each be up to 63 characters, or fewer if they contain accented letters or symbols.

The Mac doesn't appear under Network.

  • Check that Screen Sharing is on in System Settings > General > Sharing on the Mac.
  • Make sure the iPad and the Mac are on the same network.
  • Check that ScreenShare is allowed in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network on the iPad.
  • Connect by address instead: New Connection, then the Mac's name (such as Mac-mini.local) or IP address.

Why does it ask for Local Network access?

To find Macs on your network that have Screen Sharing turned on, and to connect to them. ScreenShare looks only while the Network screen is open. If you decline, you can still connect by name or address.

How do keyboard shortcuts work?

Command, Option, Control and Shift on a Magic Keyboard or any hardware keyboard go to the Mac as Mac keys. iPadOS keeps a few shortcuts for itself, such as Command-Tab; to send those, turn on Show Modifier Keys with the Keyboard in Settings, or Modifier Keys in the Keyboard menu, and use the bar.

How does touch work?

In Settings, choose Touch clicks where you tap (tap to click, drag to drag) or Touch moves the pointer (your finger moves the pointer like a trackpad). In both, tap with two fingers to right-click, drag with two fingers to scroll, and pinch to zoom.

Can I use more than one Mac at once?

Yes. In a session, choose More > Open in New Window and pick another Mac, or touch and hold a Mac in the list and choose Open in New Window. Each Mac gets its own iPad window. Disconnect closes an extra window, as Screen Sharing does on a Mac. A window you are not looking at pauses its screen updates, and only the window you are using shares the clipboard.

Is my session encrypted?

Screen Sharing encrypts your sign-in, but not the screen, what you type, or the clipboard. To encrypt everything, edit the connection and turn on Connect Through SSH. That needs Remote Login turned on in System Settings > General > Sharing on the Mac. Sign in with your Mac password, or copy the iPad's public key from ScreenShare's Settings and add it to ~/.ssh/authorized_keys on the Mac. A VPN also works.

Can I connect to my Mac from outside my home?

Yes, if the iPad can reach the Mac: over a VPN, or at an address you have made reachable. ScreenShare asks before an unencrypted connection outside your local network; use Connect Through SSH or a VPN so the session is encrypted.

How do I use the shared clipboard?

It is off by default. Turn on Use Shared Clipboard in the More menu during a session, in Edit for a connection, or in Settings for new connections. Then text you copy on one side can be pasted on the other.

Can I connect to a Windows PC?

No. ScreenShare works with the Screen Sharing built into macOS. Features that need a Mac on both ends, such as High Performance connections, file transfer and curtain mode, are not available on iPad.

  • Or buy ScreenShare Lifetime once for $39.99: no subscription.
  • Cancel the subscription at least 24 hours before it renews in your App Store account settings (Settings > your name > Subscriptions on the iPad).
  • On a new iPad, use Restore Purchases in ScreenShare's Settings.
  • Both work with Family Sharing.

Where are my connections and passwords kept?

On your iPad only. Connections are in the app's storage; saved passwords are in the iPad's Keychain and are not synced to iCloud. Delete a connection to delete its saved password. Deleting the app deletes the connections.

The app is misbehaving. What should I do?

  • Make sure you are running the latest version from the App Store.
  • Disconnect and connect again; check that the Mac is awake and Screen Sharing is still on.
  • Quit and relaunch ScreenShare.
  • If issues persist, email support@pravalabs.app with your iPadOS version, iPad model, the Mac's model and macOS version, and what you were doing.
