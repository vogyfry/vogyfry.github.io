Most requested

The Things People Ask For, Built In

What people want most from a Mac remote on the iPad: no software on the Mac, real keyboard shortcuts, more than one Mac, and a way to use it away from home.

🧩

Nothing to Install on the Mac

Turn on Screen Sharing in System Settings on the Mac and you are done. No helper app, no account, no server of ours in between.

🪟

Several Macs at Once

Each Mac opens in its own iPad window. A window you are not looking at stops asking for screen updates, and only the window in use shares the clipboard.

⌨️

Your Keyboard, Your Shortcuts

Command, Option and Control go to the Mac as Mac keys. The modifier bar sends the shortcuts iPadOS keeps for itself, such as Command-Tab.

🔒

Encrypt the Whole Session

Turn on Connect Through SSH for a connection and the screen, your typing and the clipboard all go through an SSH tunnel to the Mac's Remote Login.

Made for the iPad, Matched to the Mac

Screen Sharing.app on the Mac is the reference for what ScreenShare does; the controls follow iPadOS.

👤

Sign In as on the Mac

Use your Mac user name and password, or the VNC password if the Mac has one. Remember Password keeps it in the iPad's Keychain.

📡

Find Macs Nearby

Network lists the Macs on your Wi-Fi that have Screen Sharing on. Connect to Server takes a host name or IP address, with a port if you need one.

🖱️

Trackpad, Mouse or Touch

Hover, click, right-click and scroll with a trackpad or mouse. With touch, choose taps that click where you tap, or a finger that moves the pointer like a trackpad.

🔍

Fit, Zoom and Full Screen

Scale to Fit or Actual Size, pinch to zoom, and Enter Full Screen. A Mac with several displays shows them all, or one at a time.

👀

Control or Observe

Switch to Observe to watch without moving anything on the Mac, then back to Control.

📋

Shared Clipboard, When You Want It

Off unless you turn it on, for each connection. Copy on one side and paste on the other.

📸

Capture Screen

Save a picture of the Mac's screen to Files. The Accessibility menu opens Mission Control, App Windows or the desktop.

🗂️

Your Macs, Organised

Groups, icon or list view, Search, Open Recent, and vnc:// links, which always ask before connecting and never carry a password.

Set Up in a Minute

No app on the Mac, no account, no sign-up.

1

Turn On Screen Sharing

On the Mac, open System Settings › General › Sharing and turn on Screen Sharing.

2

Find the Mac

On the iPad, open Network and tap the Mac, or tap New Connection and type its name or address.

3

Sign In

Enter the Mac user name and password. Turn on Remember Password to skip this next time.

4

Add Another Mac

In a session, choose More › Open in New Window and pick another Mac. Disconnect closes the extra window.

What ScreenShare Is Not

ScreenShare is for Macs. It talks to the Screen Sharing built into macOS and does not connect to Windows PCs over Remote Desktop. It runs on iPad only, not on iPhone or the Mac.

Features that need a Mac on both ends, such as High Performance connections, file transfer and curtain mode, are not available on iPad. Screen Sharing encrypts your sign-in but not the screen, your typing or the clipboard; use Connect Through SSH or a VPN when that matters.
