Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

ScreenShare connects your iPad or iPhone directly to the Macs you add. Nothing you see, type or copy in it is sent to Prava Labs: there is no account, no server of ours and no analytics, and your connections and passwords stay on your device.

1. What We Don't Collect

ScreenShare does not collect, store, or transmit to us:

  • The screens you view, what you type, or what you copy
  • The names, addresses, user names or passwords of your Macs
  • Analytics or usage telemetry
  • Crash reports sent to any server
  • Device identifiers, fingerprints, advertising identifiers or location data

2. How ScreenShare Works

ScreenShare connects from your iPad or iPhone straight to each Mac you choose, using the VNC (RFB) protocol of the Screen Sharing service built into macOS, on port 5900 unless you set another. The Mac sends its screen; your device sends your keyboard, pointer and touch input. The connection goes only between your device and that Mac, or through the SSH tunnel described below; it never passes through Prava Labs.

3. What Is and Isn't Encrypted

Screen Sharing encrypts your sign-in, but not the screen, what you type, or the clipboard. On your own network that is how Screen Sharing works between Macs too. When you connect to an address outside your local network, ScreenShare asks first.

To encrypt everything, turn on Connect Through SSH for a connection (in Edit). ScreenShare then opens an SSH connection to the Mac's Remote Login and carries the whole session through it. You sign in to SSH with a password or with an SSH key that ScreenShare makes on your iPad: a P-256 key kept in the iPad's Secure Enclave where available, whose private key never leaves the iPad. The first time you connect, you confirm the Mac's SSH key; ScreenShare warns you if it ever changes. A VPN works too.

4. Local Network Access

iPadOS or iOS asks whether ScreenShare may use your local network. ScreenShare uses it to find Macs that have Screen Sharing turned on (the Bonjour service _rfb._tcp), only while the Network screen is open, and to connect to Macs on your network. If you decline, you can still connect by name or address. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network on your iPad or iPhone.

5. Shared Clipboard

The shared clipboard is off unless you turn it on, for each connection (and in Settings for new ones). When it is on, text you copy on your device is sent to the Mac, and text copied on the Mac replaces what you copied on your device. With several Macs open in separate windows, only the window you are using shares the clipboard. Clipboard text is not encrypted unless the connection goes through SSH.

6. What Is Stored on Your Device

  • Connections: the name, address, port, user name, groups and per-connection settings you choose, kept in the app's own storage on your iPad or iPhone and protected by data protection while the device is locked. They are part of the device's own backups like other app data, and are never sent to us.
  • Passwords: only if you turn on Remember Password. They are kept in the device's Keychain, available only while it is unlocked and only on that device (not synced to iCloud Keychain). You can require Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode before a saved password is used: Settings > Privacy and Security in ScreenShare. Deleting a connection deletes its saved password.
  • SSH key and SSH passwords: in the Keychain and the Secure Enclave as described above.
  • Screen captures: saved only where you choose in Files.

Hide Screen in App Switcher (on by default in Settings) covers the remote screen when you leave the app.

7. Links

A vnc:// link from another app or a web page always asks before connecting, never carries a password, never replaces a session you have open, and is not saved unless you choose Add to Connections.

8. Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking service. There are no SDKs, beacons or pixels.

9. TestFlight Beta

If you test ScreenShare through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share with us the information TestFlight collects: the email address or name you were invited with, crash reports, device model and OS version, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. We use this only to fix and improve ScreenShare, and do not share it with anyone else. Apple's collection is governed by Apple's TestFlight terms and privacy policy.

10. Subscription, Purchase and Payment

The yearly subscription, its free trial and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; we do not receive or store any payment information. The app checks your subscription or purchase with the App Store on your device.

11. Support Communications

If you contact us at support@pravalabs.app, we receive only the information you choose to include in your email, and we use it solely to respond to you.

12. Children's Privacy

ScreenShare is a utility not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

14. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at privacy@pravalabs.app.
