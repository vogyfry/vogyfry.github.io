Your Portfolio

The mechanics of a real brokerage app, with pretend money. If Apple drops 3% today, it drops in TradeSocial too.

📈

Real Market Prices

Quotes come from a live market-data feed, cached and refreshed as you use the app. The number you buy at is the number the market is showing, not a stale close from last night.

No credit card, no brokerage account, no real money. Ever.

With Apple, Google or an email address. Your $5,000 is waiting on the other side. Solo mode is free and stays free.

2

Search a symbol, buy some, watch it move. Sell when you think you should, and see whether you were right in Today's change and Total Return.

Family, friends, coworkers, a classroom. Everyone starts equal and the group finds out who was right.

Independent Budgets

Every member has their own $5,000 and their own positions. One person's bad week is their own; the group's numbers are the sum of everyone's.

What TradeSocial Is Not

It is not a brokerage, a bank or a financial adviser, and it is not regulated as one. No real money is ever deposited, traded or withdrawn, and nothing in the app is investment advice. Simulated fills are cleaner than real ones: real trading has spreads, commissions, slippage and delays, and it has the emotional weight of money that is actually yours.

The right mental model: TradeSocial teaches you what a position, a drawdown and a return feel like, at real prices, before you find out with real money.

It needs an account, because groups are people and people need to be told apart: sign in with Apple, Google or email, and delete the account from Settings whenever you like. Groups need a connection; the solo portfolio does not. Past simulation performance says nothing about real-world results. Talk to a licensed adviser before you invest real money.
