Frequently Asked Questions

What can EchoBridge do?

Two things. Translate turns what someone says into your language as they speak, and helps you answer. Practice, new in this version, lets you rehearse a conversation out loud with a partner that runs on your iPhone or iPad. Each has its own tab.

How do I translate a conversation?

  1. Open Translate and choose the two languages at the top: They speak and You speak.
  2. Tap Start. What the other person says appears in your language, and is spoken if audio is on.
  3. Answer with a suggested reply, Hold to Talk, or the keyboard button beside Start.
  4. Tap Stop when you're done.

What is the difference between Two-Way and Listen Only?

Two-Way is for a conversation: you hear them and answer back. Listen Only is for following along, such as a tour or a talk. It shows one card for each thing said and has no Hold to Talk. Suggested replies still appear; in Text Only a tapped reply opens as a card you can read out or show, with Speak as its own button.

Which headphones work?

Any Bluetooth or wired headphones your device can play music through. EchoBridge plays translations through whatever is connected. It listens with the device's own microphone, not the one in your earbuds, so keep the phone out and pointed toward the person speaking rather than in a pocket. The audio menu beside the status says where sound goes: In Earbuds, Phone Speaker or Text Only. If your earbuds disconnect mid-conversation, EchoBridge pauses rather than playing the translation out loud; tap Start to carry on.

Why do my replies come out of the phone speaker?

So the person you're talking to can hear them. When you tap a suggestion, hold to talk or type a reply, EchoBridge speaks it from the device's speaker in their language while your earbuds stay connected, then goes back to listening. To change a suggestion before it's spoken, touch and hold it and choose Edit before speaking.

Why don't I see suggested replies?

  • Suggestions appear after the other person finishes speaking, in both modes.
  • They need Apple Intelligence turned on (iPhone 15 Pro or later, or an iPad with an M-series chip or A17 Pro), in Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri in the Settings app.
  • The other person's language must be one Apple Intelligence supports. In Manage Languages, a language without it is marked Translation only.
  • Check that Suggest Replies is on in EchoBridge's Settings.

Translation and speaking work without suggestions.

How do I add or remove a language?

Tap a language name at the top of Translate, then Manage Languages. Under Available for Download, tap a language and confirm; it downloads once and works offline after that. To remove one, swipe it left under Ready to Use. The two languages of the current conversation can't be removed. iOS lets an app keep five speech languages ready at a time; adding a sixth removes the one used longest ago, and you can download it again whenever you like.

Translations arrive a moment late, or some audio is skipped

EchoBridge translates phrase by phrase, so each phrase arrives a moment after it's said. Translation Speed in Settings sets the trade-off: Faster speaks sooner in shorter phrases, More Accurate waits for the whole sentence. When someone talks faster than the voice can keep up, EchoBridge skips older audio so you hear what is being said now; the text stays on screen.

How does Practice work?

  1. Open Practice and choose the language you practice, your own language and a level.
  2. Pick a scene. Your partner speaks first, out loud, with the meaning in your language.
  3. Hold Hold to Answer, answer out loud in the language you're practicing, and let go. Your partner replies.
  4. The scene goes on until you tap End.

Stuck? Hold Help Me Say It and say it in your own language: EchoBridge shows how to say it and plays it slowly, then you say it yourself. Tap any line to hear it again, or touch and hold for Play Slower. The translate button at the top of a scene hides the meanings when you want to test yourself.

What does "You could also say" mean?

It is a more natural way to say what you just said, with a short reason. Tap it to hear it. It won't catch every mistake, so treat it as a tip, not a grade.

Why isn't Practice available?

Practice needs Apple Intelligence. Turn it on in the Settings app under Apple Intelligence & Siri. It also needs a downloaded language that Apple Intelligence supports.

Where do the scenes come from?

Scenes come in packs. Essentials (six everyday scenes) and Travel (airport, hotel, train station, taxi) are built into EchoBridge, so Practice works offline from the first launch. New and updated packs are downloaded from the App Store by iOS, without an app update. Packs are free.

What does Practice learn from my conversations?

The sentences EchoBridge said for you in Translate: the ones you could not yet say yourself. They are listed under From Your Conversations on the Practice screen, with a scene that helps you rehearse them. Remove one there, or delete its conversation in History, and Practice forgets it. Nothing about this leaves your device.

Are my conversations saved?

Yes, on this device only. Each Translate conversation is saved in History as you have it, and a new one starts when you tap Stop, tap New Conversation or choose another language. Practice scenes are kept too, in Practice History on the Practice screen. Both are left out of iCloud and computer backups. For a private conversation, turn off Keep This Conversation in History; for a scene, turn off Keep This Scene on the recap. Swipe to delete one, or delete them all. Settings > Privacy > Keep Conversations sets how long they are kept: until you delete them, 1 year or 30 days. Audio is never recorded.

What happens when I tap End?

A recap: the goals you reached, the phrases to keep (tap to hear them), how long you spoke and what to try next. The scene is kept in Practice History, unless you turn off Keep This Scene there.

What are Review and Remember Me?

Both are off until you turn them on in Settings > Practice. Keep Phrases to Review keeps your "You could also say" tips and what you asked Help Me Say It for; a Review scene then has you say them again, and one you say right leaves the list. Remember Me keeps the name you type and a few things you tell your partner in Meeting Someone or Free Conversation, so it can bring one up. Everything is listed there in plain words, to change or erase.

A tip got what I said wrong

Tap Not What I Said under it: the tip is gone, and it won't come back in Review. A word the app wasn't sure it heard is underlined with dots and never gets a tip.

What does Use My Location do?

It is off until you turn it on in Settings. With it on, Practice sets its scenes in the city and country you are in when the language you practice is spoken there, with local prices and place names, and a saved conversation notes its city. EchoBridge asks only for your approximate location and never keeps coordinates; finding the city's name is a request to Apple's servers. It is marked Beta because it is new. If Settings says location is off for EchoBridge, tap Open Settings and allow it While Using the App.

The voice sounds robotic

Download a better one. When a language only has the basic voice, a line on the Translate and Practice screens says so: tap it for the steps. In the Settings app, go to Accessibility > Read & Speak > Voices and add an Enhanced or Premium voice for the language; they're free, sound far more natural and work offline. EchoBridge uses it straight away. To choose between voices, open Settings > Voices in EchoBridge, where you can preview each one.

Does EchoBridge work on iPad and iPhone Duo?

Yes. On iPad, the transcript and your replies sit side by side. On iPhone Duo it works in every pose: closed, it's the phone layout; open flat, the transcript takes one half and your replies, Start and Hold to Talk the other, clear of the fold; half open like a laptop, the transcript is on the upright half and the controls on the flat half nearest you. Any orientation works.

Can I use the hold buttons with VoiceOver?

Yes. With VoiceOver on, double-tap Hold to Talk, Hold to Answer or Help Me Say It to start speaking, and double-tap again to send.

Does EchoBridge work offline?

Yes, once each language has been downloaded. Speech recognition, translation, voices, suggested replies and Practice all run on your device. Only Use My Location needs a connection, to name the city.

The app is misbehaving. What should I do?

  • Make sure you're running the latest version (the version is at the bottom of Settings).
  • If EchoBridge doesn't hear anything, open the Settings app, tap Apps > EchoBridge, and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition.
  • If you don't hear translations, check that the audio menu isn't set to Text Only, and turn up the volume.
  • Tap Stop, then Start again; if that doesn't help, quit and reopen the app.
  • Email support@pravalabs.app with your device model, iOS version, the EchoBridge version, and what was said and what you saw. If you test through TestFlight, you can also turn on Diagnostics Log under Settings > Developer, reproduce the problem, then tap Share Diagnostics Log and attach it. The log can include what was said, so read it first if that matters.
