2. Description of Service

EchoBridge is a conversation translator for iPhone, made by Prava Labs, LLC. It listens through the iPhone's microphone, shows and speaks a translation of what is said, suggests short replies in the other person's language, and speaks the replies you choose. Speech recognition, translation and suggestions run on the device using Apple's frameworks; language data is downloaded from Apple.

You are responsible for checking the meaning shown beside each reply before you speak it, and for anything you choose to say with the App.

EchoBridge is not a substitute for a qualified interpreter. Do not rely on it for medical, legal, financial, safety-critical or emergency communication, or to agree to anything binding.
