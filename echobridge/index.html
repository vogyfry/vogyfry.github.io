Two Tabs, One Conversation

Translate gets you through the conversation in front of you. Practice gets you ready for the next one.

Translate

Understand Them, Then Answer

EchoBridge doesn't wait for the end of a sentence. It translates in short phrases as they settle, in your headphones or from the speaker, with both texts on screen. When they finish, suggested replies appear in their language with the meaning in yours. Tap one and your iPhone says it.

Practice New

Rehearse It Out Loud

Order at a café, check in to a hotel, buy a train ticket. A partner on your iPhone speaks first, listens to your answer, replies, and shows you a more natural way to say it. As many times as you like, with no account and no daily limit.

A suggestion is a starting point, not a script. Nothing is said until you choose it.

Replies are written for the conversation so far, in polite, everyday spoken language, and kept short so they are easy to say or to tap.

💬

Tap a Suggestion

Each suggestion is a different intent, with the meaning in your language underneath. They follow the last few exchanges, not just the last sentence. Tap one and your iPhone says it.

✏️

Edit Before Speaking

Touch and hold a suggestion to change a word first: a dish, a time, a name. The meaning stays beside it, so you know what you're about to say.

🎙️

Hold to Talk

Rather say it yourself? Hold the button and speak in your language. Let go, and EchoBridge translates it and says it out loud in theirs.

⌨️

Type a Reply

For the quiet moments. The keyboard button sits beside Start: type in your language, check the translation, then tap Speak.

🔈

Spoken Where They Can Hear It

Your replies come from the iPhone's own speaker, toward the person you're talking to. Your earbuds stay connected, and EchoBridge goes back to listening as soon as it's done.

✋

Never Without You

EchoBridge never speaks a suggestion on its own. If you'd rather just listen, turn off Suggest Replies in Settings and nothing appears.

Two Ways to Translate

Switch at the top of the Translate tab, any time.

Two-Way

For a Conversation You're In

Ordering, asking the way, checking in, chatting with a neighbour. Translations in your ears, suggested replies, Hold to Talk and typed replies. Swap turns the direction round when you're the one doing most of the talking.

Listen Only

For Following Along

A tour, a talk, an announcement, the family at the next table. One card for each thing said, suggested replies, and no Hold to Talk. In Text Only a tapped reply opens as a card to read out or show, with Speak as its own button; with audio on, a tap says it from the phone.

From Your Conversations

It Learns from the Conversations You Really Had

The sentences EchoBridge said for you in Translate are the ones you couldn't yet say yourself. Practice lists them under From Your Conversations, and a scene of the same name has you say them. Swipe a phrase away, or delete its conversation, and Practice forgets it.

Use My Location · Beta

Set Where You Are, If You Want

Off until you turn it on. With it on, a scene takes place in your city when the language you practice is spoken there: its money, its street names. EchoBridge asks only for your approximate location and keeps just the city and country.

Review · Optional

Learns From What You Found Hard

Your scenes are kept on your device, in Practice History. Turn on Keep Phrases to Review in Settings › Practice, and what you asked Help Me Say It for and your "You could also say" tips become a Review scene; a phrase you then say right leaves the list. A tip that misheard you can be thrown out with Not What I Said, and a word the app wasn't sure it heard is underlined, never "corrected".

Remember Me · Optional

A Partner That Knows You

Turn it on and type your name: your partner keeps a few things you tell it, like where you're from or that you like running, and may bring one up. You see every one in Settings, change it or erase it. It never leaves your device.

Everything Happens on Your Device

EchoBridge uses the best voice installed for each language. When a language only has the basic voice, a line on Translate and Practice says so and shows the steps to a better one in Settings › Accessibility › Read & Speak › Voices.

What EchoBridge Is Not

It is not a simultaneous interpreter. It translates phrase by phrase, so you hear each phrase a moment after it's said. When someone talks faster than the voice can keep up, older audio is skipped and the text stays on screen.

Use it to get through a conversation, not to sign something. For medical, legal or emergency situations, find a human interpreter.

Practice is a partner, not a course: its tips won't catch every mistake, and your partner, like any language model, can get things wrong. It can't separate two people talking at once, and its suggestions are only as good as what it heard. It runs on iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo; there is no Mac or Apple Watch app in this version.
