Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

EchoBridge is built on a simple principle: a conversation belongs to the people having it, and nothing you say leaves your device. Speech recognition, translation, reply suggestions and Practice run on your iPhone or iPad. The audio is never recorded. Your conversations are kept on the device, in History, and nowhere else, and you can delete them at any time. Nothing you say or hear is sent to Prava Labs. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of our own.

1. What We Don't Collect

Prava Labs does not collect, receive or store:

  • Audio from the microphone, yours or anyone else's
  • What was said, its translation, the replies you chose, or what you said in Practice
  • Your saved conversations
  • Your language choices and settings
  • Your location
  • Analytics or usage telemetry
  • Crash reports sent to any server of ours
  • Device identifiers, fingerprints, or advertising identifiers

The App Store privacy label for EchoBridge is "Data Not Collected".

2. The Microphone

EchoBridge asks for the microphone and for speech recognition so it can hear what is said and translate it, and so it can hear your answers in Practice. It listens only after you tap Start, or while you hold Hold to Talk, Hold to Answer or Help Me Say It, and iOS shows its microphone indicator the whole time. The audio is processed as it arrives and then discarded. It is never recorded to a file or sent anywhere.

The people around you can hear when EchoBridge speaks, but they may not know it is listening, or that the conversation is kept as text on your device. Where the law or courtesy asks for it, let them know.

3. On-Device Processing

EchoBridge uses Apple's on-device frameworks for every step: speech recognition to turn speech into text, Apple Translation to translate it, the system voices to speak it, and, on devices with Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device language model to suggest replies and to play your partner in Practice. None of these steps sends what you say to Prava Labs or to any other server.

4. Your Conversations

Each Translate conversation is saved as text while you have it, in the app's own storage on your device, and listed in History. A new conversation starts when you tap Stop, tap New Conversation or choose another language. Saved conversations are protected by iOS data protection while the device is locked and are left out of iCloud and computer backups, so they stay on that one device.

  • For a private conversation, turn off Keep This Conversation in History. Nothing of that conversation is saved, and Practice learns nothing from it. It turns back on with New Conversation.
  • To delete, swipe a conversation in History, or delete them all. Deleting the app deletes them too.
  • They never leave the device unless you share one yourself from History, as text, with an app you choose.
  • How long: Settings > Privacy > Keep Conversations keeps them until you delete them, or for 1 year or 30 days. It applies to Practice scenes too.

5. Practice

A Practice scene runs on your device. When you tap End it is kept there, in Practice History, with the tips you were given, what you asked Help Me Say It for, and how the scene went, so you can read it again and so Practice can learn what you found hard. Like your conversations it is protected by iOS data protection and left out of backups. Turn off Keep This Scene on the recap for a scene you'd rather not keep, and delete any scene, or all of them, in Practice History.

Practice also counts what it can count honestly: scenes completed at each level, the days you practised, lines spoken. These are numbers, not words.

Remember Me is off until you turn it on in Settings > Practice. Then your partner keeps the name you type and a few short facts you told it in conversation ("likes running"), and may bring one up. Keep Phrases to Review, also off until you turn it on, keeps your "You could also say" tips and what you asked Help Me Say It for, for a Review scene. Settings > Practice shows all of it in plain words; change any of it, delete it, or Forget Everything. None of it leaves your device.

Practice learns from your saved conversations in one way: it reads the sentences EchoBridge said for you in Translate, the ones you could not yet say yourself, and lists them under From Your Conversations so you can rehearse them. It reads them from the device and keeps nothing else about you. Remove a phrase there, or delete its conversation in History, and Practice forgets it; History says so before a deletion that takes phrases with it.

A daily reminder, if you turn one on, is a notification your device shows at the time you choose; nothing is sent anywhere.

6. Location (Beta, Off Unless You Turn It On)

Use My Location in Settings is off by default. If you turn it on, iOS asks whether EchoBridge may use your location while the app is in use, and EchoBridge asks only for your approximate location. It uses it for two things: to set Practice scenes in the city and country you are in, and to note the city on a conversation saved in History.

To turn an approximate location into a city's name, EchoBridge asks Apple's servers through Apple's MapKit; that request is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and is the one thing in EchoBridge that sends anything about you off the device. EchoBridge keeps only the city and country, never coordinates, and none of it is sent to Prava Labs. Turn the setting off, or turn off Location Services for EchoBridge in the Settings app, at any time.

7. Connections EchoBridge Makes

EchoBridge itself makes no network requests to Prava Labs or to anyone on our behalf. The connections that do happen go to Apple:

  • Language downloads — the speech model and translation pack for each language you use, downloaded once when you first choose it; any Enhanced or Premium voices you add in the Settings app; and updates to the app.
  • Scene packs — Practice scenes come in packs. Two are built into the app; new and updated packs are downloaded from the App Store by iOS, as Apple-hosted asset packs.
  • The city's name — only with Use My Location on (section 6).

None of these carries anything from your conversations. Once a language is downloaded, EchoBridge works without an internet connection.

8. Diagnostics Log (Test Builds)

Builds from TestFlight have a Developer screen in Settings with an optional Diagnostics Log of timings and events, used to troubleshoot problems. It is off unless you turn it on. When on, it is written to the app's own storage on your device and can include what was said in Translate and Practice. It only reaches us if you choose Share Diagnostics Log and send it, for example to support. Clear Diagnostics Log deletes it, and turning it off stops it. Builds from the App Store do not show the Developer screen.

9. Third-Party Services and the Suggestion Server

EchoBridge does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services. There are no SDKs, no beacons and no pixel tracking.

The app can be built with the address of a suggestion server, so that reply suggestions come from a larger model than the one on your device. The builds you download from TestFlight and the App Store have no such address, and send nothing. There is no setting in the app to add one. If a future build includes one, each request for suggestions would send that server the text of the last few things said in the conversation, in both languages, and the two language names, and nothing else; this policy will say so, and name the server, before that build ships.

10. TestFlight Beta

If you test EchoBridge through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share with us the information TestFlight collects: the email address or name you were invited with, crash reports, device model and OS version, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. We use this only to fix and improve EchoBridge, and do not share it with anyone else. Apple's collection is governed by Apple's TestFlight terms and privacy policy.

11. Purchase and Payment

EchoBridge and its scene packs are free today. App Store purchases, if any are offered later, are processed entirely by Apple; we do not receive or store any payment information.

12. Support Communications

If you contact us at support@pravalabs.app, we receive only the information you choose to include in your email, including a diagnostics log or a shared conversation if you attach one. We use it solely to respond and do not share it with any third party.

13. Children's Privacy

EchoBridge is a translation and language practice tool not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

15. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at privacy@pravalabs.app.
