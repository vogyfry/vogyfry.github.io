How activation works

1 · Install

Get the app from the Mac App Store or the DMG (Windows coming soon). It starts a 3-day trial with everything unlocked — connect servers, wire up your agent, watch the savings.

2 · Buy

Mac App Store: subscribe in-app. Direct-download Mac (and Windows, once it ships): buy above and your signed license key arrives by email — usually within a few hours, always within one business day. The trial keeps working while you wait.

3 · Unlock

Paste the key in Settings → License → Apply. The app verifies it offline and unlocks on the spot — no account, no phone-home.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes — every install starts a 3-day trial with every feature unlocked. When it ends, the app pauses agent requests until you subscribe (Mac App Store) or enter a license key (the direct-download Mac edition, or Windows once it ships).

When is Toolport coming to Windows?

The native Windows tray app is built and in testing. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you the moment it’s available — when it ships, your existing Solo key will unlock it, no need to buy again.

Which AI agents work with Toolport?

Any MCP-compatible agent: Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Codex, Cursor, and others. Agents connect to one local endpoint and Toolport routes each request to the right tools across all your MCP servers.

Does my data leave my machine?

Nothing goes to Prava Labs — the registry, the embedding model, and the search index all run on your machine, with no accounts, no tracking, and no cloud processing on our side. Your requests travel only to the MCP servers you choose to connect and the web pages you ask the Retrieve module to fetch.

When are Team and Enterprise available?

Soon. Team adds a shared network registry, seat management, governance and metering; Enterprise adds air-gapped deployment, SSO, and SIEM audit export. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you at launch.

What happens when my license expires?

Licenses are verified offline on your machine. When one lapses, the app returns to the locked state until it’s renewed — your servers, settings, and data are untouched.

I subscribed on the Mac App Store — how do I switch to the direct download?

Easy, and you won’t pay twice:

  1. Cancel in System Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions — your App Store edition keeps working until the renewal date.
  2. Download the DMG now; it starts its own fresh 3-day trial, so you can confirm your local servers work before paying anything.
  3. The two editions keep separate settings, so re-add your servers and sign-ins in the new edition.
  4. Email support with your Apple renewal date and we’ll send a license key timed to start when Apple’s billing stops — no overlap, no gap.
